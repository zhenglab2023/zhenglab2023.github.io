LAB MEMBER

We are a team of biologists and computational biologists working to address key challenges in epigenetics, with a focus on understanding the cellular and molecular dynamics that underlie development and disease.

YUXUAN ZHENG（郑宇轩）, PH.D.

PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Yuxuan Zheng is the principal investigator at Human Phenome Institute / Minhang Hospital in Fudan University. He started his lab in 2023 to investigate the multi-dimensional regulatory mechanisms of development and disease with the single-cell genomics technology to better understand the origin, development, and progression of diseases. He completed postdoctoral research at Institute of Microbiology at Chinese Academy of Sciences with Professor George Fu Gao. He received his Ph.D. in Bioinformatics & Cell Biology from Peking University with Professor Fuchou Tang.
